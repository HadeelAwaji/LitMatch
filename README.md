# LitMatch — AI Book Matchmaker

A personality-driven book recommendation app available in English and Arabic.

**Live Demo:** [litmatch.vercel.app](https://litmatch.vercel.app)

---

## About

LitMatch matches readers with books based on personality, mood, and interests.
Answer 10 questions and discover your unique reader archetype along with 5 curated
book recommendations — a carefully selected mix of English and Arabic literature.

No sign-up required. Fully client-side.

---

## Features

- 10-question personality quiz
- 5 distinct reader archetypes
- 10 books per personality with shuffle
- English and Arabic with RTL support
- Book covers, page counts, reading links
- Save and share your result
- Unique themed world per personality
- Responsive for mobile and desktop

---

## Built With

- React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion
- Deployed on Vercel
